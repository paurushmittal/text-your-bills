FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Just text us — a real answer is a message away.

Do I need to link my bank?+
No. We never ask for bank logins or passwords. A photo of a bill is all we need.
How much does it cost?+
It’s free to try. You only pay if we save you money — if we don’t find savings, you don’t pay.
What bills can you help with?+
Phone and internet, car and home insurance, streaming and other subscriptions, gym memberships, and most other recurring bills. Not sure? Text it over and we’ll tell you.
How do you cancel or negotiate for me?+
Only with your OK. Depending on the provider, we’ll guide you through it, join a quick 3-way call with you, or contact the provider directly as your authorized agent. Either way, we send you the confirmation when it’s done.
How long does it take?+
We usually get back to you within a few hours with what we found. Some changes, like switching plans, can take a little longer depending on the provider.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. You decide what to share, your bills are stored securely, and we only use them to find you savings. Ask us to delete them anytime.
Can I stop anytime?+
Yes. Just stop texting — there’s nothing to cancel. Want your bills gone too? Text “delete my data” and we’ll take care of it.
